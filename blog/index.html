Fixing a 700ms PostgreSQL Query: From Seq Scan to 38ms

A chat history query sat at 700 ms p95 and kept getting worse. EXPLAIN ANALYZE showed a sequential scan and an in-memory sort; a query-aligned compound B-tree index took it to 38 ms.
