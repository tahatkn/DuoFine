Account Deletion - Common Ground

At DuoFine, we value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your Common Ground account and all associated personal data, please follow the instructions below.


Method 1: Delete Account in the App

If you currently have the Common Ground app installed on your device, you can instantly delete your account by following these steps:

  1. Open the Common Ground app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  3. Tap on the Delete Account button.
  4. Important: If you have an active Premium subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel your billing. Please ensure you cancel your subscription via your App Store or Google Play Store settings first.
  5. Confirm the final warning prompt. Your account will be securely and permanently deleted.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Web (Without the App)

If you no longer have the app installed and wish to delete your account, you can submit a deletion request:

  • Send an email to info@duofine.com.
  • You must send the email from the exact email address associated with your Common Ground account.
  • Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Common Ground".
  • Our support team will process your request and permanently wipe your data.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently and irreversibly erased from our active databases:

  • Profile Data: Your account details, email address, and profile picture.
  • Communications: All sent and received messages in your chat history.
  • Connections: Your friendships, pending friend requests, and blocked user lists.
  • Saved Items: All your bookmarked and saved locations.
  • Local Data: All cached data, search history, and security tokens on your device are cleared.

Data Retention Period

We do not apply any retention period for deleted accounts. Once the deletion process is triggered (either via the app or processed via email), the data mentioned above is instantly and permanently destroyed. It cannot be recovered.
