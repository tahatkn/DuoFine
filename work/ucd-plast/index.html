UCD Plast

An industrial B2B site for a manufacturer of rPET flakes and PET sheets — rebuilt around the buyer's path so that organic traffic turns into quote requests.

The problem

UCD Plast makes high-purity rPET flakes and PET sheets for packaging, thermoforming and industrial manufacturers, with export-ready supply. Buyers were finding the company through search — and leaving. Product information was hard to reach, the path to a quote was long, and on a phone, where a purchasing manager on a factory floor actually looks things up, the calls to action were nowhere near the thumb.

The brief was not "a nicer website". It was: make the traffic that already arrives turn into conversations with the sales team.

What we built

  • The buyer's path first. Products, certificates and contact are the whole navigation; everything a purchasing manager needs is one tap from the home page.
  • Mobile-first calls to action. The quote button lives where the thumb is, on every page, and does not disappear when the page is scrolled.
  • Rebuilt quote forms. Shorter, with the questions the sales team actually needs to answer a request — and nothing else.
  • Product pages built for search. Clear pages for rPET flakes and PET sheets, written for the queries buyers use, so organic traffic lands on the right page instead of the home page.
  • Bilingual from the start. Turkish and English, with the same structure in both, for a company that sells at home and exports abroad.

Outcome
