ShenTechin MED

A health-tech platform that turns clinical-grade data into something a person can read and act on — analysed by algorithms, supervised by a medical doctor.

The problem

Clinical data is dense by design. Sleep studies, metabolic markers and dermatological assessments are written for clinicians, and most people abandon what they cannot read. ShenTechin MED set out to bridge that gap: keep the rigour of clinical research, but deliver the result as insight a person can use tomorrow morning.

That framing puts two hard requirements on the software. The analysis has to be defensible — every conclusion supervised by a medical professional — and the experience has to be approachable enough that someone finishes the assessment instead of closing the tab.

What we built

  • An assessment flow that walks a person through tests across sleep patterns, metabolic nutrition and dermatology without ever feeling like a form.
  • AI-driven analysis across nine categories — from sleep patterns to skin type — producing a readable profile rather than a lab report.
  • Medical supervision designed into the product: the platform is designed and supervised by a medical doctor, and says so where it matters, at the moment of trust.
  • Medical insights as an ongoing surface, so the platform is a companion rather than a one-off test.

ShenTechin MED runs today as a live platform, turning complex medical data into actionable wellness insight — and it is the clearest example of the layer DuoFine works in: serious data on one side, a human on the other, and an interface that makes the two meet.
