About

Speed meets precision

DuoFine is a small product engineering studio. We take products from a rough idea to something people install, use daily and pay for — the backend that has to hold up under real traffic, and the interface that has to feel obvious.

We work in the layer most teams find hardest to staff: the join between infrastructure and experience. Query plans and WebSocket fan-out on one side; onboarding flows and empty states on the other. A product only feels fast when both are right.

Everything we ship is measured. The numbers on this page come from production systems we run or have handed over — and one of them is written up in full.

How we work

  1. Discover

    We map the product, the hard problem at its centre and the numbers that will define success. You get a scoped plan and an honest estimate.

  2. Build

    Working software every week, not slides. Backend, app and infrastructure grow together so nothing is integrated at the end.

  3. Ship & measure

    Store releases, monitoring and the first weeks in production are part of the job. We stay until the numbers say it works.

Questions we get asked

What do you build?

Web and mobile products end to end — backend, app and infrastructure — plus performance rescues that make an existing product fast again.

How does an engagement start?

With a message describing what you are building. You get a reply within one business day, usually with questions, and a call if it makes sense. That conversation costs nothing.

Who owns the code?

You do. Repositories, store accounts and infrastructure are set up in your name from day one.

Where are you based, and do you work remotely?

Türkiye (UTC+3). Everything we do is remote-first, and we have shipped for clients across time zones.

Do you take fixed-scope work?

Yes — audits, performance rescues and well-defined builds. Larger products run in short iterations with working software every week.

Products in production

Three shipped platforms, each with a different hard problem at its centre.

Health-tech · Web platform

ShenTechin MED

A health-tech ecosystem merging advanced algorithms with clinical research, supervised by medical professionals.

Problem
Clinical-grade health data is dense and intimidating; people abandon what they cannot read.
Approach
An assessment flow supervised by doctors, with AI-driven analysis across sleep, metabolic nutrition and dermatology.
Outcome
A live platform that turns medical data into actionable wellness insight across three health domains.
  • AI-driven analysis
  • Clinically supervised
  • Three health domains

Consumer · iOS & Android

Common Ground

A platform that brings people together under the most optimal, fun and efficient conditions based on where they are.

Problem
Meeting across a city means negotiating place, time and transport — and as the message table grew, chat history slowed to 700 ms.
Approach
React Native + Django with real-time WebSockets, a meetup-point engine that weighs location, distance and transport, and query-aligned compound indexes for chat.
Outcome
Chat history at 38 ms p95, live in the App Store and Google Play.
  • React Native + Django
  • Real-time WebSockets
  • 38 ms p95 chat load
Read the case study
Industrial B2B · Website

UCD Plast

A comprehensive industrial B2B platform for a manufacturer of rPET flakes and PET sheets.

Problem
Organic traffic that arrived and left: buyers could not find products or a quick way to ask for a quote.
Approach
Rebuilt around the buyer's path — mobile-first calls to action, shorter quote forms, clear product pages built to convert search traffic into leads.
Outcome
Conversion rose sharply; the platform placed Top 5 of 10,000+ entries at the Corporate LiveWire Innovation & Excellence Awards 2026.
  • Industrial B2B
  • Organic lead conversion
